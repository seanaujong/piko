Piko needs to be allowed on the pages you read

Piko waits for you to drag a hyperlink. To notice that, it has to already be running on whatever page you are reading when you do it — which is why this is one permission covering every site rather than a prompt each time.

Chrome will ask you to confirm. Nothing happens until you do, and you can withdraw it at any time from chrome://extensions.

What Piko does with it

  • Notices a dragged link, and fetches that page so it can be shown in reader mode.
  • Reads the text of a page only once you have dragged a link or pressed the toolbar icon. Until then it adds nothing to the page at all.
  • Saves a sentence to your journal when you click it. That journal stays on this device.

What it will not do

  • Run on webmail, password managers, chat, or sign-in pages. That exclusion is enforced by Chrome, not by Piko's own code.
  • Send cookies with the pages it fetches, so it never sees a signed-in version of anything.
  • Reach addresses on your own network — your router, your intranet, localhost.
  • Send anything anywhere. There is no account, no analytics, and no server.

Piko is free and open source. If you would rather grant access to particular sites only, you can do that from chrome://extensions instead — Piko will work on the sites you choose and stay inert elsewhere.

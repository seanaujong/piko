Depths and Shallows

The tide is the rise and fall of sea levels caused by the combined effects of the gravitational forces exerted by the Moon and the Sun, together with the rotation of the Earth. Tides originate in the oceans and progress towards the coastlines, where they appear as the regular rise and fall of the sea surface.

Tide tables can be used for any given locale to find the predicted times and amplitude of the tides there. The predictions are influenced by many factors, including the alignment of the Sun and Moon, the phase and amplitude of the tide, and the shape of the coastline with its near-shore bathymetry. Harbour depths vary with them, and records the range.
